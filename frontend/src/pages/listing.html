About Me

こんにちは！ Hello! I'm NORIKO, a certified Japanese language teacher with over 8 years of teaching experience. I'm passionate about helping students achieve their Japanese learning goals, whether you're a complete beginner or looking to perfect your advanced skills.

My Teaching Style

I believe in creating a comfortable and engaging learning environment. My lessons are tailored to your individual needs and learning pace. I use a variety of materials including textbooks, authentic materials, and multimedia resources to make learning Japanese enjoyable and effective.

What I Offer

• Structured lessons for all levels (JLPT N5-N1)
• Conversation practice for fluency building
• Business Japanese for professionals
• Cultural insights and real-life Japanese usage
• Customized materials and homework
• Exam preparation (JLPT, EJU)

My Background

I hold a Master's degree in Japanese Language Education and am a certified Japanese Language Teaching Professional. I've taught students from over 40 countries and understand the challenges learners face. My goal is to make your Japanese learning journey smooth and rewarding!
